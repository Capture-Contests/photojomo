Capture Ghana invites photographers, filmmakers, documentarians, and visual storytellers to explore one of West Africa’s most historically significant and culturally vibrant destinations.

Along the Heritage Coast, centuries of history meet everyday life — in the harbours, the markets, the workshops, and the celebrations that continue to shape the country today.

A Journey Through
History, Culture & Discovery

Stretching along Ghana’s southern coastline, the Heritage Coast is home to remarkable landmarks, thriving communities, breathtaking landscapes, and stories that have shaped generations.

From the UNESCO World Heritage Sites of Cape Coast and Elmina to the sacred grounds of Assin Manso, the rainforest canopy of Kakum National Park, vibrant fishing communities, local artisans, musicians, chefs, historians, and entrepreneurs, Capture Ghana celebrates both the nation’s rich heritage and its living, evolving culture.

This is a place where visual storytelling becomes a bridge — between past and present, between visitor and community, between curiosity and authentic human connection.

Why Capture Ghana

More Than a Contest

Capture Ghana is a destination storytelling initiative that brings together creators, communities, tourism partners, and cultural institutions to build a lasting visual archive celebrating Ghana’s people, heritage, and future.

  • Celebrate Ghanaian culture

  • Preserve heritage through visual storytelling

  • Increase international destination visibility

  • Support local communities and creators

  • Connect global audiences with authentic Ghanaian experiences
