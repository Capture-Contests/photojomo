The Mike Charles Invitational

Photographer, producer, and creator of “Guyana: Yours to Discover.”

This initiative celebrates exploration, cultural connection, and environmental storytelling through photography and film.

Honoring the legacy of Lt. Col. Michael Charles

The Mike Charles Invitational is a curated photography and film initiative celebrating the spirit of exploration, humanity, and environmental beauty throughout Guyana. Through visual storytelling, the Invitational inspires photographers, filmmakers, and creatives to document the country’s landscapes, cultures, and untold stories while fostering a deeper connection to the people and places that define Guyana.
