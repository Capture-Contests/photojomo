The Caribbean’s Most Influential Island.

Loud, Layered, and Impossible to Ignore.

Jamaica has given the planet its music, its philosophy, its pace & yet its mountains, its interior parishes, its street vernacular, and its deep Maroon history remain largely uncaptured by the global lens. This is the invitation to change that.

The Maroon Trail Initiative

In Partnership with the Accompong Maroon Community

Honouring the oldest free Black community in the Western Hemisphere through a dedicated photography expedition into the Cockpit Country, a landscape of limestone karst hills and centuries of sovereign resistance.

A Heritage That Refused
to be Erased

The Maroons of Accompong signed a peace treaty with the British Crown in 1739 making them one of the first groups in the Caribbean to win official recognition of their freedom and sovereignty. Their culture, language, and ceremonies persist intact in the hills of St. Elizabeth.

Capture Jamaica is honoured to work alongside the Maroon Council to bring these stories to a global audience without extraction or reduction.

The annual Accompong Maroon Festival held each January remains one of the most significant and least-documented cultural events in the entire Caribbean. We are here to change that.
