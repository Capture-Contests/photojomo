Global Creator Standards

Creator Guidelines

These are the global standards we hold for every creator participating across the Capture Contests platform.

  1. 01

    Create Authentically. Tell Stories Responsibly.

    Capture Contests celebrates human creativity and the power of authentic visual storytelling.

    Our global platform brings together photographers, digital video creators, filmmakers, visual storytellers, and emerging creative voices from around the world. These guidelines establish the core standards we expect from creators participating across the Capture Contests ecosystem.

    Specific eligibility, entry, technical, and submission requirements may vary by contest, destination, or experience. Always review the applicable Official Rules before participating.

  2. 02

    Authentic Human Creativity

    Capture Contests exists to discover, celebrate, and elevate authentic human-created visual work.

    Submitted photography, digital video, film, and other eligible visual content should reflect the creator’s own creative vision and authorship.

    Content that is fully generated by artificial intelligence and presented as original creator-captured work is not permitted in Capture Contests competitions unless expressly stated otherwise for a particular experience.

    Digital tools may be used for normal creative development and post-production where permitted, but creators should not use technology in ways that materially misrepresent the authorship or authenticity of submitted work.

  3. 03

    Original Work

    Creators should submit work they have created and have the right to enter.

    You are responsible for ensuring that your submission does not knowingly infringe another person’s copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other intellectual property rights.

    Where another person’s protected material is substantially incorporated into your work, you are responsible for obtaining any permissions required for its use.

  4. 04

    Ethical Storytelling

    Great visual storytelling carries responsibility.

    We encourage creators to approach people, communities, cultures, traditions, environments, and destinations with respect, curiosity, and integrity.

    Creators should avoid intentionally deceptive representation, exploitation, or harmful stereotyping and should respect culturally sensitive subjects, locations, traditions, and circumstances.

    Authenticity should never come at the expense of human dignity.

  5. 05

    People, Privacy & Permission

    When photographing or recording people, creators are responsible for understanding and complying with applicable laws and obtaining permissions or releases when required.

    Additional releases or documentation may be requested from selected creators, finalists, or winners when necessary for publication, exhibition, licensing, promotion, or other authorized uses.

    Creators should exercise particular care when creating work involving minors, vulnerable individuals, private settings, or culturally sensitive circumstances.

  6. 06

    Respect for Places & Communities

    Capture experiences are intended to encourage discovery—not disruption.

    Creators should respect local laws, community standards, private property, protected environments, cultural sites, wildlife, and restrictions governing photography, filming, drones, or access to particular locations.

    Do not endanger yourself, another person, wildlife, property, or the environment for the purpose of creating an entry.

  7. 07

    Creator Ownership

    Your work remains your work.

    Participation in Capture Contests does not automatically transfer ownership of your original photography, digital video, film, or other eligible creative work to Capture Contests.

    Individual contests and experiences may require creators to grant specific licenses or permissions necessary for judging, promotion, publication, exhibition, archival presentation, media distribution, or other stated uses.

    Those rights will be defined in the applicable Official Rules, participation terms, or separate agreements.

  8. 08

    Editing & Creative Integrity

    We recognize that editing and post-production are part of contemporary visual storytelling.

    Reasonable adjustments and creative treatments may be permitted depending on the contest or category. However, editing should comply with the requirements of the applicable competition and should not be used to circumvent authenticity requirements.

    Where required, creators may be asked to disclose significant manipulation, compositing, generative alteration, or other material changes to submitted work.

  9. 09

    Verification

    Capture Contests may request additional information or original source material to verify the authenticity, authorship, eligibility, or technical integrity of a submission.

    Depending on the competition, this may include original image or video files, RAW files, metadata, higher-resolution files, releases, identification, eligibility documentation, or other supporting information.

    Failure to provide requested verification may affect eligibility.

  10. 10

    Technical & Submission Requirements

    File formats, resolution, dimensions, file sizes, duration, entry limits, categories, deadlines, and other technical requirements are established for each individual contest or experience.

    Please refer to the applicable contest page and Official Rules for current requirements.

  11. 11

    Creator Conduct

    Creators participating in Capture Contests experiences are expected to interact respectfully with fellow creators, communities, partners, judges, staff, and members of the public.

    Fraud, plagiarism, deliberate misrepresentation, manipulation of eligibility or voting systems, harassment, or attempts to circumvent competition safeguards may result in disqualification or other action under the applicable Official Rules.

  12. 12

    A Global Community of Visual Storytellers

    Capture Contests is building more than a place to enter competitions.

    We are creating opportunities for creators to discover places, document cultures, share perspectives, develop their craft, connect with communities, and contribute to a growing global body of authentic visual storytelling.

    Every creator brings a different perspective.

We want to see
the world through yours.
