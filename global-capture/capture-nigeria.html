Capture Nigeria invites photographers, filmmakers, documentarians, and visual storytellers to explore one of Africa’s most dynamic and influential nations. From vibrant cities and thriving creative industries to remarkable landscapes, rich traditions, and extraordinary communities, creators are invited to tell authentic stories that celebrate Nigeria’s people, culture, innovation, and enduring spirit.

A Nation of Creativity, Culture & Possibility

Nigeria is a country of extraordinary diversity—home to hundreds of ethnic communities, vibrant traditions, world-renowned music, groundbreaking cinema, celebrated cuisine, thriving entrepreneurship, and some of Africa’s most dynamic cities.

Capture Nigeria invites creators to explore beyond the familiar, discovering authentic stories through meaningful connections with the people, places, and experiences that make Nigeria one of the continent’s most inspiring destinations.

Every journey reveals another story waiting to be told.

Why Capture Nigeria

More Than a Contest

Capture Nigeria is a destination storytelling initiative that brings together creators, communities, tourism partners, cultural institutions, and local organizations to build a lasting visual archive celebrating Nigeria’s remarkable diversity and global influence.

  • Inspire meaningful travel

  • Celebrate Nigerian creativity and culture

  • Increase destination visibility

  • Preserve traditions and heritage

  • Empower creators and local communities

  • Connect global audiences through authentic storytelling
