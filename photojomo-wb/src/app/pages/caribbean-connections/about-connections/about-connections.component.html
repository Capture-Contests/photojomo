Connecting the Caribbean World.

Caribbean Connections is the community and legacy arm of Capture Caribbean — created to build lasting connections between the Caribbean, its people, its diaspora, and those around the world drawn to its cultures and stories.

Our Purpose

The Caribbean is not one story.

Across its islands, nations, coastlines, and communities exists an extraordinary diversity of histories, cultures, identities, traditions, and perspectives.

Caribbean Connections was created to provide a place where those worlds can meet.

A place for Caribbean people at home and abroad, creators, travelers, cultural enthusiasts, and the simply curious to discover one another and develop a deeper relationship with the Caribbean world.

Why Connections

Capture Caribbean may introduce someone to a destination, creator, photograph, film, or story.

Caribbean Connections is designed to continue that relationship.

Rather than allowing the connection to end when a competition, campaign, or journey concludes, we are building a community through which relationships can grow over time — across destinations, cultures, disciplines, and generations.

The Third Path

Caribbean Connections completes the Tri-Path of Capture Caribbean:

Awareness

Sweepstakes introduce people to the Caribbean.

Engagement

Challenges and competitions invite people to participate.

Legacy

Caribbean Connections turns participation into lasting community.
